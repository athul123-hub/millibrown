Why the UN Is Not Intervening in Russia's Aggression

1. Veto Power in the UN Security Council

Russia, as a permanent member of the UN Security Council, can veto any resolution, including military intervention or formal condemnation.

2. Limits on Use of Force

The UN Charter only allows military action with Security Council approval or in self-defense. Russia's veto prevents any authorization of force.

3. Symbolic General Assembly Actions

The UN General Assembly has passed non-binding resolutions condemning Russia, but they have no legal power to enforce change or intervene militarily.

4. Role of Regional Alliances

Organizations like NATO and the EU have taken the lead in supporting Ukraine since the UN cannot act decisively due to structural limits.

5. Fear of Escalation

Direct UN military involvement could escalate into a wider or nuclear conflict, so most action has focused on sanctions, diplomacy, and aid.
